## 研究问题

Does a previously listed and byte-verified Pages artifact remain retrievable across a later observation cut merely because its successful upload, workflow-run status and deployment log persist, and can a current newer artifact serve as a bounded positive listing control

## 可证伪假设

- 支持条件: the exact prior artifact ID is listed in the fixed 2026-10-25 record but absent in the 2026-10-26 same-run listing, with successful run/job history unchanged and a newer artifact listed at the same current cut
- 推翻条件: the older artifact remains listed on the current cut, the prior record refers to a different artifact/run, or the newer supposed positive control is also absent
- 归因限制: even if the transition is observed, retention-days=1 and expires_at cannot alone prove that scheduled expiry rather than another removal mechanism caused it
