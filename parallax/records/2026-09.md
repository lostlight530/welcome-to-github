## AGI-scale epistemic integrity

Parallax 下一阶段不研究 `某模型是不是 AGI`.

研究对象升级为 `长期自主 Agent 如何在不断变化的世界中保持可追溯且不越界的判断`.

这要求把当前已有的 evidence discipline 扩展到.

- multi-agent delegated evidence chain
- tool and retrieval provenance
- world-state drift during long deliberation
- model/system configuration identity across toolset, memory, reasoning effort and harness changes
- evaluator independence and result ownership
- aggregate recovery versus per-object completion
- correction chronology across continuously updated public systems

这些方向当前是 frontier, 不是新的 CASE 支持或 NOTES finding.
