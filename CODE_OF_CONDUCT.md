# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This code applies to repository Issues, pull requests, reviews, Discussions, and other public collaboration around this project.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- communicate professionally and respectfully
- focus criticism on claims, evidence, code, methods, and repository decisions rather than individuals
- distinguish observed facts, external claims, inference, proposals, and unknown state
- correct material errors openly and preserve provenance when a historical record must remain auditable
- respect privacy, security boundaries, attribution, copyright, and third-party licenses
- avoid spam, harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation, fabricated evidence, or knowingly misleading validation claims

## Research and engineering integrity

Do not present unexecuted checks as passed, unobserved behavior as observed, or historical evidence as current state. Do not conceal material conflicts, failures, missing data, or uncertainty merely to make a result appear cleaner.

## Sensitive information

Do not publish credentials, private communications, personal data, unpublished exploit details, or other sensitive material. Follow `SECURITY.md` for security-sensitive reports. Conduct concerns containing private or sensitive information should be raised through an available private contact route rather than a public Issue.

## Moderation

The repository maintainer may edit, hide, remove, lock, or reject contributions that violate these standards or materially undermine repository integrity. Repeated or serious violations may result in participation restrictions.

The repository owner retains final moderation and merge authority.
